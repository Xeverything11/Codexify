Colors

Codexify has an extensive color system, with 15 shades across 7 hues plus black and white, giving 107 colors to work with.

List of colors

Each hue meet 7:1 contrast ratio (WCAG AAA for all text) for <= 300 on 900 to black, <= 100 on 800, white on 700 and vice versa.

Each hue meet 4.5:1 contrast ratio (WCAG AA for all text, AAA for larger text and graphics) for <= 500 on 980 to black, <= 400 on 900 to 950, <= 300 on 800, <= 200 on 700, white on 600 and vice versa.

Each hue meet 3:1 contrast ratio (WCAG AA for larger text and graphics) for <= 600 on 990 to black, <= 500 on 800 to 980, <= 400 on 700, white to 200 on 600 and vice versa.
