Tables

Codexify provides opt-in table styles due to widespread usage of third-party plugins.

Overview

Table styles are opt-in due to the widespread usage of third-party plugins and widgets. Add .table class to <table> elements to style the tables.

App Users Revenue

Pro Calculator 678,901 $345,678
Floral Journal 2,345 $3,210

</table>

Variants

You can add .table-<variant> to either a cell or row of the row, or the entire table.

Class Heading Heading
Default Cell Cell
Progressive Cell Cell
Destructive Cell Cell
Error Cell Cell
Warning Cell Cell
Success Cell Cell
Notice Cell Cell 
<!-- On tables -->
<table class="table table-progressive">...</table>
<table class="table table-destructive">...</table>
<table class="table table-error">...</table>
<table class="table table-warning">...</table>
<table class="table table-success">...</table>
<table class="table table-notice">...</table>

<!-- On rows -->
<tr class="table-progressive">...</tr>
<tr class="table-destructive">...</tr>
<tr class="table-error">...</tr>
<tr class="table-warning">...</tr>
<tr class="table-success">...</tr>
<tr class="table-notice">...</tr>

<!-- On cells (`td` or `th`) -->
<tr>
  <td class="table-progressive">...</td>
  <td class="table-destructive">...</td>
  <td class="table-error">...</td>
  <td class="table-warning">...</td>
  <td class="table-success">...</td>
  <td class="table-notice">...</td>
</tr>
