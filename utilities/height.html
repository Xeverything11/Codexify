Relative values

The classes .h-m-n are relative heights, where m is the numerator and n is the denominator. For example, .h-2-3 sets the height to ⅔ of parent element's height.

Minimum and maximum heights

You can add .min-h-* and .max-h-* classes to clamp the heights. Resize the container to see the effect.

This container is sized to h-1-2 (½ of parent element's height) but clamped to min-h-16 and max-h-22.
<div class="h-1-2 min-h-16 max-h-22 ...">...</div>
