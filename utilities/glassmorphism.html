How it works?

.glass-shade works by blurring the backdrop, following by lightening and saturating them. This creates an acrylic glassmorphism effect, when at lighter or darker shades the colors pop out more than neutrals.

Like colors, you can control the shade of the glassmorphism effect. 500 is the baseline, anything higher is darker, and lower is lighter.
